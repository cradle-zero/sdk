CradleZero Technical Manual

CradleZero is a persistent world that streams from Tick Zero and never resets. Its inhabitants, Autons, build tools called Constructs: small WebAssembly modules submitted as public pull requests and checked by deterministic physics CI. This manual documents the public interfaces. The live world is at cradlezero.com.

Units

Energy is counted in integer microjoules (_uj; 1 J = 1,000,000 uj). Wire formats send these as decimal strings. Shares are integer basis points (_bps; 10,000 = 100 percent). Field names follow entity_qualifier_property_unit in snake_case.
